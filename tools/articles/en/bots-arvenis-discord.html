Bots are a well-run server's best allies: they welcome, moderate, automate and entertain while the team focuses on what matters. On my projects, I use the bots developed by Arvenis, the collective I run. Here is what each of them does.

Manage BOT

Management · Moderation · Security

The all-in-one bot to manage and secure your Discord server. It's the foundation of a well-supervised community:

  • Moderation: the tools to enforce the rules.
  • Security: protection for the server and its members.
  • Modmail: a private channel so members can contact the team.
  • Automation: repetitive tasks handled without any staff involvement.

Écho BOT

Voice management

Simple, automated management of your private voice channels. Members get their own channels and choose who can join, without the team having to deal with it.

  • Private channels: personal voice channels for members.
  • Automation: the channels manage themselves.
  • Access control: everyone decides who enters their channel.

Warden BOT

Administration · Protection

A versatile bot to administer, protect and automate your server. It covers protection, moderation, administration and automation: everything you need to keep a server clean and under control day to day.

Hiboursier BOT New

Economy · Casino · Clans

A complete economy bot, with seasons, a casino, clans, heists and much more. Ideal for giving members a reason to come back every day:

  • Economy: balance, bank, jobs, stock market, companies and loans between members.
  • Casino and games: slot machine, roulette, blackjack, poker, races, duels and lottery.
  • Clans: shared treasury, leaderboard and clan wars.
  • Heists: plan a heist as a team, or put a bounty on a member.
  • Seasons: season leaderboard and hall of fame, with quests and achievements.
  • Progression: levels, jobs, shop, items and marriage.

Which bot for which need?

  • Manage and secure your server: Manage, backed up by Warden for administration and protection.
  • Organise voice channels: Écho.
  • Engage and retain your community: Hiboursier.
